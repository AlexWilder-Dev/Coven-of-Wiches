Compiled 19 October 2026 (a Monday, and they're closed Mondays). Everything below has a source. "Unconfirmed" means I couldn't check it.

## The Brixton unit and where it sits

- **14D Market Row, London SW9 8LD** (postcode from Meat Free Monday, HappyCow and OSM addresses of neighbouring units; Time Out's SW9 8PS is Brixton Village's Granville Arcade address).
- Market Row (Wikipedia): designed 1928 by Andrews and Peascod, "links Atlantic Road, Coldharbour Lane and Electric Lane. The interior is double-height and windows in the roof provide light." Grade II listed since 2010.
- OpenStreetMap shows Market Row as a T: a long east–west arcade from Electric Lane to Atlantic Road, plus a short arm south to Coldharbour Lane. OSM has a unit polygon tagged `addr:housenumber=14D` (still named after an old tenant) on the **west side of that Coldharbour Lane arm**, about 12m in from the street. The Brixton Village pin sits at the top of the same arm.
- **Opposite: Esme's** (organic greengrocer, 16A). OSM puts it across the arm, and "ESME'S RO…" and "…C & HERBAL PRODUCT LTD" show reflected in the Coven's own window photos.
- Next door: Arepa & Co (14B–C, Brixton Village map) and a black PIRATE.COM hanging sign, both visible in their photos. HENGE Brixton is at 14E.
- Outside: black trestle tables and benches splattered with yellow paint, a faux-ivy pillar, paper lanterns and festoon lights strung across the arcade. At night the arcade glows electric blue and the pink unit next door goes magenta (Time Out photo).
- **The Linktree's "GOOGLE MAPS & OPENING TIMES" link still resolves to Wood Street, Walthamstow** (51.5884, -0.0048). Anyone following it ends up in E17.

## How they write

- Shop sign: "SANDWICHES, PICKLES & BOOZES, OH MY!" Window: "666% VEGAN 'WICHCRAFT" (Annabelle quotes it). A-board: "DIVE BAR / CRAFT BEER / VEGAN FOOD". Door chalkboard before opening: "14D / THE COVEN LIVES!! / AUGUST 19".
- Linktree bio: "Bitchin' 'Wiches, Made By Witches?"
- Statement (Brixton Village listing): "THE COVEN of WICHES is London's best loved Vegan Sandwich Restaurant and Bar by Chef Annabelle Clarke (Mildred's, Alter, Facing Heaven). Witchcraft and Occult themed as a Feminist reclamation of the term "Witch" as a patriarcal gender slur, The Coven is a safe space for all peoples and a haven for those who wish to do no harm. The food is good too."
- Menu language: ALL CAPS, puns in the nouns rather than in sentences: vastrami, vish, chkn, letty, baconnaise, shampain, potatty, gloop, DEATH JAM, "synthetic truffle shroom", "edible glitter", "cauldron side". Double exclamation marks.
- Annabelle (Meat Free Monday, 29 Sep 2026) is blunt, funny and a bit lyrical: "We don't really." / "Tasty food is just tasty food." / "If no, then it probably needs a little salt." / "Crowd pleaser, always." / "More sandwiches and more compassion, more education and more hugs – love always wins in the end."
- Customers are "our amazing Coven". Sandwiches are "'Wiches".

## What reviewers praise and complain about

Praise: the Reuben, above everything ("the best vegan Reuben", "outrageous", "beyond words"). Portions ("HUGE", "generous to a new level"). Value. Messy in a good way. Friendly, fast staff ("Busy but still quick!", jamesthomsonn, Oct 2026). Mac and cheese. Time Out (Sep 2026): "This culty vegan deli, pickle house and bar is lots of fun… Pure wichcraft." Infatuation (Walthamstow, Mar 2025): "a cross between a Halloween store and the kitchen at your witch core-obsessed friend's house", with Meat Loaf on the record player.

Complaints: hard to find or out of the way. Walthamstow was "hidden down one end of Wood Street Indoor Market" (Infatuation), "Tucked inside the market" (London Vegan), "a little out of the way if you aren't local". Other complaints: tiny, few seats, closed Mon–Tue, "More sandwiches please!", "could do with a bit less sauce", lots of vegan cheese on the Philly, most sandwiches contain soy.

## Who comes when

- **Lunch is the rush.** Their own board says "PEAK TIME 12-3". Infatuation tags it "Lunch, Dining Solo". Time Out: "Best for a swift sarnie". A Saturday-lunch reviewer found it busy for three hours.
- **Evenings are invisible.** They open Wed–Fri 5–9 and Sat till 10 as a "dive bar", but not one review or press piece describes an evening visit. All the coverage is sandwiches at lunch.
- People make trips: "Worth the trip!", "worth going out of your way". Brixton locals are glad it's closer: "Will be here more often now they're a lot closer to us in Brixton!" Vegans bring sceptical meat-eaters (Annabelle).
- Dogs: "dog-loving" and dog bowls at Walthamstow. **Not confirmed for Brixton.**

## Design reasoning

- **Yellow is the brand.** The shopfront, the menu board (#F5EE36) and the logo tile (#FFF200) are all yellow. Black and white come from the logo's brush letters and drop shadow. That makes three colours, all theirs. No purple, no green.
- **Night blue for the evening.** Market Row is lit electric blue after dark (≈#3444F8 in their Time Out photo). The after-5 mode moves into that blue, so lunch looks like the yellow front in daylight and the evening looks like the arcade at night.
- **Type, from their own surfaces:** the logo is used as an image, extracted from their board. **IM Fell English SC** is the closest free match to the inky old-style caps on the menu board, so it carries dish names and prices. **Anton** matches the heavy condensed caps of the "DIVE BAR / CRAFT BEER" A-board and carries labels and the open/closed sign. **Archivo** is a plain grotesque for reading.
- **Texture:** the yellow paint splatter from their black benches, drawn as SVG. The pentagram/witch watermark is genuinely on their board, so a faint version sits behind the board section, as on the original. Nothing else is occult decoration.
- **Layout:** the first screen works like the shopfront and the board: logo, an open/closed sign that knows about the 3–5 break, this week's 'wiches with prices, and "opposite Esme's, off Coldharbour Lane". No hero photo. Photos come next to the thing they show.

## What the owner most wants this site to do

1. Put the weekly board (dishes and prices) one tap from Google. Right now it lives in Instagram posts and a Drive PNG behind Linktree, and Annabelle sells the Reuben by swiping photos on her phone.
2. Get people to 14D, inside a market. Walthamstow was "hidden down one end" and "out of the way", and the Linktree map link still points to Walthamstow. Brixton needs "off Coldharbour Lane, opposite Esme's, look for the yellow".
3. Fill Wed–Sat evenings. The dive bar (Wiccan Liquor on keg, frozen margs, pickleback and kimchiback shots, the Gravity Well fridge) gets no coverage, while lunch is already "PEAK TIME 12-3".
